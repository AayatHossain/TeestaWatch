View

A normal camera sees clouds in the monsoon. Radar sends its own signal and sees through them.

How it is measured

  1. Sentinel-1 radar, VV band, calibrated to decibels (dB).
  2. Speckle smoothed in a 5 × 5 pixel window (100 m).
  3. Water = pixels darker than the threshold. Calm water reflects the signal away, so it looks dark.
  4. New water = water after, but dry before.
  5. Area = pixels × 20 m × 20 m.

Risk rule

High: bank closer than 400 m, or arriving within 2 years.
Medium: closer than 1 km, or arriving within 5 years.
Low: everything else.
